How to use these puzzles with your child

Choose a familiar category such as animals or fruit. Ask your child to name the object before moving it, then invite them to notice its outline. After they complete a match in the app, say the word together and look for a similar object at home or outdoors. Short, relaxed sessions give children time to try each shape for themselves.

If your child is new to matching, begin with a few recognizable objects and offer a hint about one feature, such as a long neck or round wheel. As they become more comfortable, switch categories and ask what makes two shapes different. These are parent-led activity ideas, not a promise of a particular learning outcome.
